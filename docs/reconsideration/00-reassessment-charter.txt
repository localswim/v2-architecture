FRESH-CHOICE REASSESSMENT CHARTER - 2026-10-25

Authority and decision rule
Terry clarified that a functioning v1 is evidence of needs and useful behavior,
not an exact-copy mandate. Every product/technical choice may be reconsidered,
including prior v2 recommendations and the language split. A change needs a
concrete case: user problem, benefit, losses, complexity, migration cost and
evidence that would distinguish alternatives. Retention needs a case too.
Prior approval preserves history; it gives no candidate immunity from challenge.

Scope and evidence
- Architecture recommendations only. No implementation, installs, live changes,
  trust provisioning, data migration, releases or operational reconfiguration.
- Preserve the public/MIT FOSS commitment, private operational data, and UTF-8/LF.
  Board operating instructions remain in force while working on this proposal.
- Examine public v2 architecture documents as candidate designs, not constraints.
  Public v1 README behavior documentation may identify actual user needs; no
  legacy source/tests/internal design or real board data. Do not claim isolated
  clean-room certification; prior source exposure remains disclosed.
- No requirement to preserve exact syntax, layout, storage, quirks or formulas.
  Do not silently add roadmap features or claim Terry adopted a proposed break.
- Browse primary official sources for new technical/version/ecosystem claims.
  Cite direct URLs; no manufactured benchmarks or unexecuted prototype claims.

Output rule
Each author writes one <=900-word note plus at most two small tables. Inventory
the important decisions in the assigned scope, distinguish observed need from
assumption, compare 2-3 serious approaches where meaningful, and give retain /
change / unresolved recommendations. Record losses, transition burden and the
smallest useful validation. A gap in evidence is a result; do not force a choice.
Keep high-level, avoid protocol/package/schema design and new gate bureaucracy.
Question accumulated complexity as well as legacy constraints. No agent edits
another author's artifact, board, repo, README or operating instructions.

Independence and handoff
One card = one worker turn. No grandchildren. Fresh review agents author none of
their inputs. Review checks the case for recommendations, cross-scope effects,
coverage and unsupported facts; it is not another consistency-only acceptance.
Synthesis produces a decision agenda, not adoption of unapproved alternatives.
Only Terry controls Completed. Finished worker artifacts go RFR. Material fixes
need separately bounded correction cards; preserve original evidence.
