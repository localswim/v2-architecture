Observation, evidence and batching
Committed-revision signals, periodic reconciliation and optional filesystem watches
are wake-up hints. A write, rename or notification is not an attributable event:
atomic replacement, coalescing and metadata writes defeat write/event count equality.
Never inspect card prose to classify a wake-up or already queued alert.

Recommend an authorized activity reader over committed audit metadata: stable
board/storage identity, history epoch, opaque ordered cursor, revision, verified
principal/actor class, action category and operation correlation. Exclude content,
credentials, attachment names/bytes and arbitrary paths. Submitted actor labels do
not establish attribution. Imported/unverified actors remain unknown.

Capture an upper cursor after the quiet window; query all pages in (saved, upper]
with explicit complete/gap/expired status. Cursors survive host generations and
bind board identity, history epoch and filter semantics; timestamps are insufficient.
Validate envelopes and continuity before suppression. New activity beyond upper
starts the next batch. Never prefilter human/unknown events from suppression evidence.
A healthy poll with unchanged upper and no outstanding hint has no batch; empty
evidence for an observed or queued interval still alerts. Debounce restarts on hints,
with a bounded maximum batch age
to prevent starvation. Overflow, epoch change, missing history, watcher loss or
invalid cursors or missing checkpoints create an uncertainty interval, never an
empty-success shortcut.

Persist observation watermark, activity checkpoint, unresolved gaps and pending
alert separately. Close a covered interval only after a durable suppression decision
or confirmed adapter acceptance. Acceptance proves the adapter's stated boundary,
not human receipt. A delivered gap warning does not prove missing history consumed;
retain its unresolved marker and resume from an authoritative new baseline explicitly
reported as discontinuous. Persist pending intent before sending; crash recovery can
repeat a window and must not silently discard it.

Attribution / delivery outcomes
Evidence or result | Required disposition
Complete nonempty interval; every event verified as configured automation principal | Suppress; persist bounded skip/checkpoint; no card inspection, work resumption or alert.
Human, mixed or unknown actor | Queue sanitized change notice to captured target; preserve Terry's changes.
Empty, malformed, failed, partial, expired or discontinuous activity query | Queue uncertainty notice; no suppression or invented attribution.
Adapter confirms acceptance | Record receipt/disposition and covered checkpoint; never claim read or task completion.
Adapter proves not accepted | Retain pending; bounded backoff/retry with same delivery identity.
Timeout, crash or ambiguous adapter result | Retain unknown; reconcile if supported. No exactly-once claim; possible duplicates remain explicit.

Ownership and continuity
One host-owned monitor scope per physical board aggregate uses verified exclusion,
captured child identity and fresh generation under #0011. Its private lock, status,
target and pending/checkpoint records are distinct from every other board. Monitor
scope never acquires board writer ownership or edits canonical state. A heartbeat,
PID or generation alone cannot authorize takeover. After host loss, prevent new
effects; successor must prove predecessor quiescence and reconcile started delivery
before activating that scope. Otherwise show unavailable delivery, preserving pending.

Keep the originally configured conversation target across restart/upgrades. Reload
validates configuration, persists progress, quiesces old effects and acknowledges
replacement; unsupported reload uses cooperative stop/start. Explicit authorized
retarget changes future batches only; pending batches retain captured target unless
explicitly migrated after in-flight reconciliation. Stop persists pending/checkpoints,
acknowledges quiescence, then releases scope. Deadline expiry reports incomplete
shutdown; no PID-based forced cleanup or optimistic release.

Activity reader / monitor | Authenticated read-only committed metadata, stable cursor/upper bound, pagination completeness and retention/epoch evidence; no card-detail dependency.
Delivery adapter / monitor | Configured existing conversation integration; board-scoped private target reference, stable delivery identity, sanitized notice, accepted/not-accepted/unknown and optional outcome lookup. No new notification platform.

Decisions, tradeoffs and limits
Recommend durable pending plus conservative alerts over memory-only simplicity;
this costs private runtime storage and can duplicate crash windows. Prefer committed
signals plus polling to filesystem-only monitoring, which misses attribution. Bound
queues, retries and diagnostics; exhaustion remains visible, never silently drops
pending work. An adapter lacking reconciliation cannot promise duplicate-free delivery;
any duplicate-tolerant unknown retry needs explicit configured policy and disclosure.
Liveness checks distinguish responsive owner, progressing activity reader and delivery
health; quiet boards need not send alerts. Exact budgets, cursor retention and adapter
qualification remain specification choices. Terry's review decides this proposal;
no missing owner input prevents handoff, and future RFR work is not preapproved.
