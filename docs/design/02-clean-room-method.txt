Practical worker workflow
1. A requirements editor converts selected evidence into a small behavior packet: user-visible outcome, inputs/outputs, error semantics, acceptance examples, unresolved choices, provenance IDs and explicit exclusions. Do not include old filenames, classes, control flow, algorithms or implementation-specific failure workarounds unless Terry separately accepts a necessary observable requirement.
2. A separate reviewer checks traceability and leakage against approved evidence. Label the packet proposed until Terry accepts its scope and unresolved required choices. Acceptance establishes a requirements baseline; it does not transform source-derived evidence into isolated provenance.
3. Give implementation workers only the accepted packet, new design decisions, approved third-party documentation and permitted assets. Log the packet revision and worker exposure declaration. A fresh context helps enforce input boundaries but cannot substantiate isolation if its packet carries prior source-derived knowledge.
4. Derive new acceptance checks from the packet and synthetic examples. Record ambiguities for owner resolution rather than consulting legacy tests or source. Keep implementation choices attributable to new design reasoning.
5. On disallowed exposure, stop the affected work, record the material viewed and impacted outputs without redistributing it, and ask the primary to scope disposition. Preserve evidence; do not silently relabel or claim independence. Resume only within an approved disposition.

Evidence ledger / provenance record template
Record ID; title; date; recorder; source type and exact reference/section; source revision or digest where available; access scope; source-derived/exposure flag; observed fact versus inference; proposed behavior requirement IDs; exclusions and uncertainty; permission/owner decision reference; reviewer and review result; accepted packet revision; downstream artifact IDs; generator/dependency lineage when relevant; supersedes record ID.

Maintain append-only decision history. Corrections supersede records; missing revision data is explicit. Store concise evidence extracts separately from worker packets. Do not place credentials, real card prose or private data in the ledger. Trace each accepted requirement and generated artifact back through this chain.
