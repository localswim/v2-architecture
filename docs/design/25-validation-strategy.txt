Representative acceptance journeys (maximum nine)
1. Race two authorized edits and stale human acceptance; verify one conflicting
   commit, coherent state/audit/outcome and retained losing intent. Spoofed human
   labels, unknown/underprivileged principals and cross-board access refuse without
   domain mutation or successful-change history; valid human controls succeed.
2. Lose response/cancel across admission and replacement; reconcile retained matches
   across restart, reject changed fingerprints, and keep absent/expired/damaged retry
   evidence indeterminate. No automatic first submission, new identity or replay.
3. Compete alias-path hosts and explicitly stopped-board CLI; then crash/drain with
   surviving children. Native exclusion and proven quiescence precede successor
   readiness; stale PIDs/listeners/generations cannot authorize takeover.
4. Inject invalid references/format, disk-full and each persistence fault; verify
   proven base/candidate or fenced unknown, unchanged preserved evidence, stale warm
   reads versus cold quarantine, and independent healthy-board availability.
5. Exercise wrong root/key/name, untrusted issuer, hostile/null/missing Origin,
   foreign authority, missing header/cookie/context, expired/consumed grants and
   revoked sessions/streams. Test anonymous bootstrap's narrow exception separately;
   protected reads/events require32 binding, fresh login never replays intent.
6. Interrupt/oversize upload, alter digest, race permissions and unsafe paths, then
   remove a covered attachment. Verify complete-byte admission, atomic cover clearing,
   inert download/preview and retained current/pinned/unresolved bytes; no guessed GC.
7. Navigate cards/boards/Back/Forward during search, edit and upload; late responses
   cannot retarget intent or overwrite dirty drafts. Verify unavailable routes,
   independent preferences, keyboard move/search/drawer focus and screen-reader
   announcements. Memory-only mode promises no crash/reload draft recovery.
8. Disconnect Git, race manual refs/staged work, lose push acknowledgement and interrupt
   flush. Local saves continue; exact pinned inventories preserve unrelated files;
   commit/push/restore-readiness remain distinct, uncertain effects reconcile first.
9. Query bot-only/human/mixed/unknown/empty/malformed/failed/gapped paginated activity;
   suppress only complete nonempty verified bot-only intervals without card inspection
   or write/event-count equality. Crash/reload/retarget retains checkpoint, pending
   target and delivery uncertainty; adapter acceptance proves neither receipt nor
   exactly-once delivery. Inspect sanitized diagnostics and visible exhaustion.

Evidence and exit gates
Simulation validates decisions and fault classification, not OS exclusion/durability.
Native process/filesystem campaigns establish only named OS/filesystem combinations;
process termination does not qualify power-loss survival. Real-browser evidence
records selected stable Edge/Chrome/Firefox versions, assistive technology and actual
trust setup; synthetic DOM/automated scans cannot replace interaction walkthroughs.
Windows11 x64/Linux x64 remain candidates until individually qualified.

Before implementation reliance, specify retention/outcome/cursor horizons, pin release,
draft confidentiality, capacity, freshness/invalidation, leases and shutdown budgets.
Measure03's provisional workload/latency/accessibility targets with synthetic datasets,
sample counts and failures; negotiate changed budgets explicitly, never imply success.
Terry retains retention/privacy, recovery-point/time, hardware-loss and platform scope.

Qualify22's pinned Go dependency graph, isolated TS5 generator package, TS7 browser
checking and asset builder together. Metadata availability is not compatibility.
Fixture the supported OpenAPI3.1 subset across Go/browser validators, generated wire
types and serialization; unsupported constructs fail explicitly. Verify regeneration,
clean-build comparison, packaged assets, notices and provenance before release claims.

Recommend layered gates over browser-only end-to-end checks: broader evidence costs
more but exposes native/trust blind spots. Legacy differential testing is excluded.
Exit requires traceable results, resolved material findings, fresh independent review
and Terry's acceptance of limitations; architecture approval authorizes no execution,
install, migration or release. No missing input prevents this proposal's handoff.
