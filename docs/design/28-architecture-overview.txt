Responsibilities and invariants
One host serves independent board engines with bounded resources; a host crash is a
shared failure domain. Workspace discovery provides navigation, never board rights or
cross-board atomicity. Go alone owns domain policy, authoritative order/counts and
commit outcomes. Browser and CLI validate envelopes and preserve intent; neither can
make actor labels authoritative. All writers, including maintenance, share admission.

Stable identities survive renames/archive. Archive preserves content, comments,
relationships and history. Human acceptance binds authenticated capability plus the
reviewed card and policy revisions, atomically with transition/evidence; subsequent
substantive edits cannot inherit acceptance. Assignment grants no authority.

Blocking, nonblocking associations and hierarchy have separate semantics. Blocking
cycles are exposed; completion stops reachability/urgency, while archived unfinished
cards still participate. Adopted graph recommendations include acyclic single-parent
hierarchy, self-link refusal, distinct downstream counts and forward urgency promotion.
One persisted priority changes through explicit edits or atomic promotions; removing
constraints never automatically demotes it. Floors/scores/order are derived from one
committed revision.13 supplies opaque ASCII identities and bytewise final tie-breaking,
clarifying30's previously deferred representation; clients use server order.

Canonical deterministic JSON co-commits state, attributable metadata audit and operation
recognition. Bytes are complete and verified before references commit. Candidate,
intent, replacement, synchronization and read-back distinguish committed, proven
noncommit and indeterminate outcomes. Unknown storage outcomes fence writes; invalid
cold starts quarantine, while warm retained reads are visibly stale. Evidence is
preserved; no empty replacement or automatic backup promotion. Audit metadata cannot
reconstruct edited prose or establish tamper-proof authorship. Logical read-back and
process-crash recovery do not establish power-loss or hardware-loss survival.

Operation identity equals correlation within stable principal/aggregate scope.
Authorized retained fingerprint matches return historical outcomes without execution;
changed content conflicts. Missing, expired or damaged retry evidence stays
indeterminate. Navigation, cancellation, timeout, reauthentication and restart never
justify replay or automatic identity replacement. This is bounded recognition, not
global exactly-once execution.

Physical aggregate exclusion covers aliases and bytes through a stable anchor;
logical board IDs alone cannot establish ownership. Startup recovers before serving.
Drain closes admission and proves storage/child quiescence before ownership release;
deadlines, PIDs, listeners and generations cannot justify takeover. Unknown worker
effects disable affected scopes until reconciled; demonstrably safe local work may
continue. Closing a browser does not stop the host.

Trust and residual decisions
32's accepted bootstrap recommendation assigns issuer/control plane, launcher, Go
adapter and browser explicit roles: verify captured instance chain/name/expected key
before disclosing a single-use grant; exchange at the selected TLS origin; bind
protected reads, commands and streams to session cookie, memory token and browser
context. CLI/workers have distinct grants. Revocation/lease expiry and quiescence
precede replacement; login never replays intent.

Local-issuer provisioning expands account trust and remains separately authorized
and unqualified. Actual issuer restrictions, browser-profile trust and localhost
resolution must be tested. Host-only cookies are not port-isolated; never-reused
instance names, keys and exact origins carry isolation. Human issuance assumes trusted
launcher access unavailable to automation; TLS, Origin and browser presence prove no
person and exclude no account-controlling process. Trusted desktop account,
cooperating writers, directly attached storage and qualified loopback remain the
envelope; remote hosting and hostile-account isolation are outside it.

Browser memory retains drafts/journals across navigation, not reload/crash. Durable
private recovery needs explicit confidentiality, retention and authenticated rebinding.
Numerical performance/capacity, freshness, shutdown/lease, outcome/cursor horizons,
pin release, attachment/audit retention and recovery objectives remain unverified.
Terry retains platform/browser scope, privacy, full content-history needs, hardware-loss
tolerance and compatibility choices before dependent execution. These future gates do
not prevent architecture handoff. Material contradictions found in29 would require
bounded correction before final acceptance.

Qualification and adoption handoff
25 covers all eleven quality scenarios through nine representative journeys. Record
requirement/decision revision, fixture digest, exact tools/target, expected/observed
result and limitations; unqualified cells stay visible. Process termination cannot
substitute for power-loss evidence; compiler metadata cannot substitute for a build.

26 requires isolated storage, ownership, origins, credentials, repositories and monitor
paths/targets. Approved private copy rehearsal, named mapping/loss report, complete
independent backup/restore proof and separately authorized freeze/cutover precede one
new authority. Include duplicate logical IDs, stale destinations and post-cutover
writes. Rollback requires compatible release/storage/trust, drained writers/workers,
reconciled external effects and fresh credentials. Restoring an older copy can lose
later writes; preserve both histories/bytes and obtain an explicit loss/recovery
decision. Copying, successful parsing and push acknowledgement transfer no authority.
