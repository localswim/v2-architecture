LOCALSWIM REBOOT: CANDIDATE PRODUCT SCOPE
Card #0001 | 2026-10-25 | Author: sol_medium_0001_product_scope
Status: proposal for independent #0004 review and Terry's acceptance.

Proposed boundary
A local-first board product for human and automation collaboration, with configurable
workflow, concurrent browser/CLI use, durable attributable changes, and multiple
independent boards. Evaluate Go for core/service/CLI/background tools and TypeScript
7.x for the browser. This is a product boundary, not an approved storage, protocol,
process, security, migration, or toolchain design.

Evidence and provenance
The r2 input brief and explicit user directions are primary evidence [B]. The narrow
public README sections below document current behavior; they do not approve its
retention or establish implementation guarantees. Earlier source exposure is known;
this note claims no isolated clean-room certification. No source, tests, internal
architecture, private board contents, or peer outputs were consulted for this note.

Compatibility versus new requirements
Propose continuity of stable card references, workflow meanings, attribution,
non-destructive archive, privacy-conscious reports and mutation outcome semantics.
Exact CLI syntax/JSON shapes, routes, storage schema, bookmark format, limits,
timings, retention duration and historical quirks are undecided compatibility
contracts. No importer, exporter or cutover is authorized here.

The mandated new direction is Go plus TypeScript 7.x evaluation. Do not quietly
substitute a TypeScript major; #0022 must verify availability/channel. Broader
identity models, remote collaboration and stronger durability are possible new
requirements, not demonstrated approvals.

Assumptions and material limits
- Assume local browser plus trusted automation is the initial audience, pending
  owner confirmation of platforms, users, exposure and workloads.
- Audit metadata is not necessarily replayable content history: README description
  and rename events omit old/new prose [R2]; full reconstruction needs a decision.
- README crash-safety statements do not promise acknowledged writes survive power
  loss [R4]. Do not turn them into an unqualified durability guarantee.
- JSON/Git-friendly storage is a candidate requirement; mechanisms remain open.

Alternatives and recommended owner decisions
1. Prefer the capability baseline above over a minimal cards-only first release;
   it preserves the documented collaboration workflow but increases delivery scope.
   Terry may stage attachments, workspace, publishing or monitoring explicitly.
2. Approve behavioral continuity first; exact legacy compatibility increases
   migration convenience and constrains replacement design. Select what must match.
3. Confirm attachment retention/limits, triage formula, audit reconstruction needs,
   supported platforms/workloads and durability budgets before dependent design.
4. Accept evidence/reuse rules through #0002/#0004 before downstream work.

Non-goals
No implementation, installs, repository scaffolding, live changes, migration,
release or cutover. No inherited Python module layout, exact routes, process
count, polling cadence or source-reload mechanism. No cloud hosting, mobile app,
multi-tenant SaaS or new notification destination commitment.
