Owner requested customer-visible milestones toward V1 feature parity, decomposing only M01; accepted Spike 01/local scope and explicit owner policies remain.

Milestone goals

M01 - Work across projects and boards in one local workspace
Open one localhost workspace, select any of three synthetic boards across two projects, see truthful counts for supported Attention categories, use shared pins, and switch/reload without losing or misapplying private work. Missing lane categories are explicitly not configured until M02.
Decomposed now; synthetic data only.

M02 - Complete daily card work through browser and CLI
Reach the remaining documented daily card outcomes: usable card detail and committed history, search/filter, rename, hierarchy and typed links, archive/unarchive, configurable workflow and keyboard-friendly interaction. Preserve explicit priority and human acceptance.
High level only; define detailed acceptance and any deliberate V1 differences before implementation.

M03 - Manage attachments and cover images in ordinary use
Add, inspect, download and remove attachments and choose/clear covers through ordinary product controls; bytes, attribution, history, references and export/restore remain consistent.
High level only; no attachment-storage rewrite or new limits selected.

M04 - Operate the local tool and its optional automation reliably
Restore useful monitor alerts with bot-only suppression, explicit targets and delivery state, optional Git-readable publishing/backup and maintainable local update/restart behavior. Board saves remain independent of optional delivery/publishing.
High level only; explicitly opt-in external publishing/notifications, no message sending or remote credentials authorized now.

M05 - Bring a V1 board into V2 without losing its work
Demonstrate read-only inventory/dry-run and isolated JSON-to-SQLite import with card IDs/tickets, actors, policy, relationships, history and exact attachment bytes accounted for; prove coexistence and a usable recovery path.
High level only; synthetic rehearsal first. Named real sources/destinations and any activation/cutover require later explicit owner authorization.

M06 - Accept feature parity and a maintainable everyday release
Reconcile the V1 capability matrix against delivered behavior and owner-approved intentional changes; demonstrate an ordinary multi-board browser/CLI workflow, restart/recovery, backup/restore and optional operations on the final candidate.
High level only; no full-parity claim until evidence closes every retained outcome and the owner accepts it.
