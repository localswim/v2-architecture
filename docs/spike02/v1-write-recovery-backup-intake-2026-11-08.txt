Design boundaries
- A validated exact terminal refusal remains refused even when a later read fails. Ordinary HTTP failure/refusal is not automatically a terminal proof. Current state/history and exact-original outcome inspection are distinct.
- Original-retention tests apply to supported operation kinds and their exact contracts. They do not silently broaden M16 recipient retention or reopen Completed scope; any new retention mechanism needs separate design and scope authority.
- A drawer close, navigation, stopping the wait or local warning dismissal does not abort the admitted POST, settle UNKNOWN, cancel/roll back a write or authorize a blind replacement. Private evidence deletion, if supported, separately discloses lost knowledge.
- Preserve submitted prose A exactly while newer editable draft B remains separate. Prove identity, authority, generation, bases, bytes, selection and focus at the applicable surface.
- Require a brief coherent capture and perform expensive verification/status/staging/commit/push outside primary writer/transaction admission. Test real write completion while each expensive stage is deliberately held. No specific queue, filesystem layout, snapshot strategy or timing constant is selected.
- Captured reference/inventory membership and verified bytes must remain coherent during concurrent uploads/removals. Later committed edits remain eligible for a later backup, and a partial artifact cannot be reported as valid.
- Analyze arbitrary Git hooks, fsmonitor and external filters against the chosen approach, including dependencies on omitted files. Support compatible operation or safe refusal before destructive/staging actions; preserve safeguards and unrelated worktree/index state.

Qualification and disposition
- Use real browser and actual CLI paths with native effect/audit assertions. Clearly label controlled response/commit/storage/Git barriers and retain exact candidate/tool/fixture/source bindings. Follow the shared queue, synthetic ownership and cleanup workflow.
- Distinguish mutation acknowledgement, current saved state, backup freshness/local commit/push status and browser transport health. One must not stand in for another.
- Existing contracts remain gates for the next separately authorized applicable regression. Feature-specific snapshot/push cases first run in M27/M28. Original-target effects, UNKNOWN, native joins and current-authority fences remain intact.
