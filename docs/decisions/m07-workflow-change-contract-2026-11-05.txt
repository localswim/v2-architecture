M07.01 EXISTING-BOARD WORKFLOW CHANGE CONTRACT R1
2026-11-05 | Proposal for owner acceptance | #0096

Outcome and authority
Edit an existing default or configured board through its ordinary browser and
actual CLI, with an exact preview, deliberate save and retained policy history.
This proposal and its synthetic companions require fresh independent Sol / High
review and Terry's acceptance before #0058 implementation. Specification is not
runtime evidence. Terry approved the M07/M08 split and M07 development start.
Companions are ../spike02/m07-workflow-change-acceptance-r1.txt and
m07-workflow-change-fixture-r1.json. The accepted M06 contract governs unchanged
behavior. This contract explicitly supersedes its immutable-policy restrictions
only for the supported existing-board changes below.

1. Supported edits and fixed meaning
Support label changes, lane-array reorder, ordinary transition/actor changes,
ordinary-right ceiling changes, new waiting lanes, and empty waiting-lane
retirement. Submit a complete replacement candidate, not a sequence of patches.
An existing default with legacy CURRENT card/approval facts incompatible with the
edited-profile invariant cannot take its first real edit. It receives the explicit
WORKFLOW_INCOMPATIBLE refusal described below and keeps its valid legacy behavior.
Validate the complete resulting graph before any mutation. Retain the M06 bounds
of 4..16 active lanes, 3..64 edges, 64 KiB definition, exact Unicode labels,
case-folded label uniqueness, sorted unique rights/actors/edges and human
reachability. Definitions never contain executable predicates or scripts.

The initial, in_progress, review and completed lanes keep their original IDs and
roles forever. Existing waiting lanes keep their IDs and role. Changing any
existing lane's identity or role, changing the workflow identity/profile,
replacing a required-role lane, merging lanes or automatically remapping cards
is refused. A rename keeps identity. Reordering changes presentation only.
Retire a waiting lane only when NO current card, including an archived card,
occupies it. Historical snapshots, activities, originals and approvals may still
refer to it. Retain its ID in history and forbid reusing that ID in any later
policy, even with its old label. Reusing an old label for a different active lane
is allowed if current labels remain unique. Clients must display identity/role
context so equal labels across versions do not imply continuity.

Keep exactly the M06 protected review edges, in_progress->review and
review->completed, with human admitted on both and human alone on the latter.
Only the former may also admit automation when its ceiling allows handoff.
No other edge enters review/completed and no edge leaves completed. The human
read/read-own-outcome/create/edit/review-handoff/approve rights remain mandatory.
Both actor kinds retain read/read-own-outcome. No automation approve right,
owner inference, automatic start or new terminal/reopen operation is introduced.

All supported changes leave cards in their current lanes. No card revision,
content, current approval pointer, explicit P0..P5 priority, owner, parent,
relationship, comment, attachment or archived flag is changed by a policy edit.
Existing cards may become unable to take an old ordinary edge or lose automation
access. Show this in preview. Required human reachability prevents a policy-only
dead end. An archived unfinished card must still be deliberately unarchived
before workflow movement. Completed stays terminal/read-only on every card path.

2. Default and configured identities
Configured boards retain the M06 Workflow shape, workflowId and UUID lane IDs.
Workflow.revision becomes a positive safe integer and equals board.policyVersion.
Revision1 and its exact stored definition are retained. profile is omitted for
configured definitions. Explicit profile:"configured" is refused to avoid a
second canonical spelling.

An untouched default board retains workflow omission, policyVersion1, all four
literal lane IDs and old shapes. Its first real edit introduces Workflow with
profile:"default", one freshly generated workflowId, revision2, and the same
lanes identified by their original strings. The required ID/role pairs are
Ready for Work/initial, In Progress/in_progress, Ready for Review/review and
Completed/completed. These IDs remain exact even after their labels change.
New waiting lanes use UUID IDs. No conversion of current or historical lane
values is performed. profile:"default" is the only new profile spelling and
appears only after an explicit existing-default edit. The workflowId is generated
once in the draft, retained through preview/save/UNKNOWN and fixed after commit.
The implicit revision1 default has no invented historical UUID or workflow field.
Before the first real default change, validate ALL current cards, including
archived cards, against the strict current-state invariant. A noncompleted card
has no current approval pointer. A completed card has a pointer to an existing
human approval for that card, issued under policy1, with reviewed card revision
equal to current revision minus1. The approval's required references must resolve.
Legacy state accepted by the old complete validators can fail this additional
editing gate. Return WORKFLOW_INCOMPATIBLE for that case during preview/save,
after ordinary authority/CAS/candidate checks and before any domain effect.
For a frozen save, this is the normal durable NOT_COMMITTED refusal carrying
that code. Preview remains read-only. This gate checks current pointers and
their relevant approval facts, not every unrelated retained historical approval.
This is a supported-feature limitation, not a declaration of corrupt legacy
storage. Keep source opening, legacy operations, export and restore behavior
unchanged. Do not clear pointers, repair Completed, invent approvals, or make
historical bytes conform to the newer invariant. An exact NO_CHANGE candidate
does not enter the new profile and does not require this additional gate.
After the first committed edit, every CURRENT card uses strict current lane/
approval validation on open, read, mutation admission and restore, even when its
unchanged state pin is still implicitly1. The permissive legacy branch applies
to historical records only. A pin1 current card cannot conceal later corruption.

For default-profile definitions, transition endpoints and lane IDs accept those
four literal IDs plus waiting UUIDs. For configured definitions they remain UUIDs
only. Sort transitions by exact endpoint strings. All other M06 validation rules
apply through the same profile-aware validator. M06 new-board init/create keeps
its original configured-only UUID/revision1 grammar and rejects profile.
The complete identity is board/incarnation, profile, workflow identity when
present, policyVersion, and lane ID. A label, array position, ticket or a matching
UUID in a second board never supplies authority.

After a default edit, preserve card.edit.lane="In Progress" as its exact legacy
start intent against that stable ID, regardless of display label. Do not add new
values to legacy lane. Extend explicit card.edit.laneId to the profile's exact
lane IDs, as its only input field. This allows ordinary moves to added waiting
lanes and fixed default IDs through card move. Untouched defaults still reject
laneId as in M06. Configured boards still reject legacy lane. Archived/review/
completed/equal-target refusal order and NO_CHANGE rules otherwise remain M06.

3. Retained policy and historical interpretation
Retain an immutable complete policy record for EVERY committed policy version.
Each record is {policyVersion:N, workflow?:Workflow}. Default version1 omits
workflow, configured version1 contains its exact original Workflow. Later records
contain complete definitions. Versions are contiguous from1. Only the current
pointer advances. Definitions and retired IDs are never rewritten or pruned by
card-history/activity/outcome retention. No-change creates no policy version.
Bound retained history to 128 policy records and 8 MiB of canonical UTF-8 encoded
workflowPolicies array. Count version1. A changed save exceeding either bound
returns INVALID_INPUT before any domain mutation. NO_CHANGE remains available
at capacity. Preview exposes current use and remaining capacity. Do not silently
drop old policies, auto-compact, or publish a state exceeding these limits. These
limits are an explicit M07 choice, alongside existing transport/archive limits.

Add optional workflowPolicies to Board, complete board/card/history/search
results and lane-bearing hierarchy results. It is omitted for untouched M06
boards. After the first edit it is the ordered complete policy record array
including version1 and current. Existing workflow is the exact current definition
and board.policyVersion names it. Inconsistent duplicate representations refuse
at storage/read/archive validation. Current and history arrive in one coherent,
authorized selected-board snapshot. Partial card search never grants whole-board
or mutation authority. A failed/incomplete definition read gives unavailable
context, never guessed labels from a catalog or another window.

Add optional workflowPolicyVersion to Card and SnapshotState. Omission means1
and is retained exactly for old facts. On each new card revision after policy1,
write its current policy version. A policy-only edit does not repin existing
cards. When capturing a prior card revision into a snapshot, copy its existing
pin, not the policy current at snapshot insertion. History labels/roles resolve
the state's pin. Current-board/card displays resolve CURRENT policy while exposing
the recorded pin separately where history is shown. This keeps a card created
before a rename historically intelligible without rewriting that card's facts.
Pruned state has no invented lane/label. Retain available summary provenance.
Historical default-policy1 facts retain the complete old default validation
branch even after the first edit. Do not send those facts through configured or
post-edit approval validation merely because the board now has a Workflow.
In particular, preserve baseline-accepted approval associations and opaque
legacy snapshot JSON with no lane. Where a legacy snapshot has a lane, retain
the old default-literal membership checks. The old branch's permissiveness is
historical compatibility and supplies no current approval or prerequisite proof.
Snapshots pinned to2 or later, and all configured history, use their respective
strict retained-policy validation. A missing pin on old default history means1.

Opaque storage acceptance does not imply representability in SnapshotState or
the existing archive. Preserve exact stored bytes and the old default opaque-state
validation through physical upgrade, policy edit and reopen. Preserve existing
bounded history-read/export unavailability when those old bytes cannot pass the
applicable full read/archive validators. Do not add a new opaque encoding, invent
readable card fields, silently omit the record or publish a lossy archive. Legacy
facts already representable by the complete old API/archive remain representable
and byte/value-conserved through those paths and repeated restore.
Activity records for policy changes carry policyVersion and workflow identity,
not copied card prose. Historical card activity/originals are not reinterpreted
as accepted under current policy. Retention of activity is not a substitute for
the policy table or explicit state pin.

4. Approval, prerequisites and actual authority
Every new approve request supplies CURRENT policyVersion, now any positive safe
integer. Existing approval records retain their exact issuing policyVersion and
reviewed card/board revisions. Old approval originals still decode/refreeze with
their original value and bytes. A stale approve cannot silently adopt a new policy.

An existing current approval remains valid across ALL supported workflow edits
because required lane identities, roles, review path, human-only approval and
the reviewed card revision remain invariant. Validate its issuing retained policy,
human attribution, card identity, reviewed-card-revision=current-card-revision-1,
current approval pointer and current completed-role membership. Do not require
approval.policyVersion == CURRENT policyVersion. The exact issuing version must
exist and must authorize the original review/approval facts. An old version's
existence alone does not excuse malformed/missing approval evidence.
For default-policy1 historical approval evidence, preserve the old validators'
acceptance rather than retroactively applying this current-authority test to
every retained historical record. Only approvals independently meeting the
strict current test can satisfy a current Completed/prerequisite assertion.
The same rule MUST govern blocking-prerequisite admission, reopen, archive and
restore, for both default and configured boards. A nonarchived completed peer
with that valid approval still satisfies a source-owned active blocking link
after a policy edit. Other link types and hierarchy remain irrelevant to start.
Existing substantive card mutations retain their approval invalidation rules.
Missing/corrupt historical policy is an integrity refusal, never approval or
permission inferred from a current label. Role/identity changes that would require
global invalidation are outside the supported contract and are refused.

workflow.edit requires the authenticated ordinary channel's CURRENT edit grant
AND current actor-kind edit ceiling in the writer snapshot. Browser uses its
verified local human actor. Actual CLI uses its existing automation actor. Neither
may supply actor kind or borrow native-owner authority. Assignment gives no right.
The edit ceiling is the chosen policy-management admission right for this bounded
feature. There is no separate enrollment, grant-admin or credential operation.

Ceilings never issue, expand, revoke, replace or rewrite stored grants. Effective
ordinary authority is intersection of an independently valid current grant and
the current policy ceiling. Lowering a ceiling may immediately disable an existing
grant right. Raising it may make that independently retained right effective
again, but cannot create a missing or revoked grant. Preview discloses both effects.
Stored historical and disabled grant rows may legitimately exceed CURRENT ceiling.
Validate their legal vocabulary/actor restrictions independently, not against the
latest ceiling. New grant issuance retains current-ceiling admission. Forbidden
automation approve/grant-admin/history-admin rights are invalid in every
historical/current record. Existing separate automation restore administration
retains its accepted native-channel rules and cannot enter actorRights.
Native-owner administrative separation is unchanged. Automation may remove its
own edit ceiling. Subsequent ordinary automation edits refuse. Human mandatory
edit plus its independently valid grant remains the supported recovery path.

5. Mutation, preview, concurrency and originals
Add command workflow.edit with target none and normal exact board/incarnation/
opId/expectedRevision/expectedCardRevision:null envelope. Route is
PATCH /api/v1/boards/{boardId}/workflow. input is exactly
{expectedPolicyVersion:N,workflow:COMPLETE_CANDIDATE}. The candidate revision is
N+1 and expectedPolicyVersion is a positive safe integer. Unknown keys, explicit
nulls, duplicate JSON keys, oversize definitions and malformed integers refuse.
The exact wire body contains opId, incarnation, target:null, expectedRevision,
expectedCardRevision:null and input. The authenticated route supplies boardId.
Do not add apiVersion or boardId to this ordinary mutation body's grammar.
The required envelope nulls above are the only null exceptions. Bound each full
preview and mutation request to 800000 raw UTF-8 bytes, preserving the current
board-API limit in addition to the independent 65536-byte definition limit.
Oversize bodies retain existing HTTP413/nonterminal handling before freezing.
Result is exactly {workflowId:UUID,policyVersion:NEXT}. Add workflow policy
FactRef kind with id=workflowId and revision=NEXT, and workflow.edit activity type.
Preserve fingerprintVersion1 and every preexisting command's canonical bytes.
New command fields participate in its canonical original. No mutable preview
token, current-state lookup or normalization is inserted during freezing.

Add read-only POST /api/v1/boards/{boardId}/workflow/preview, with body
{apiVersion:1,boardId,incarnation,expectedRevision,expectedPolicyVersion,workflow}.
It requires current read and edit authority and current exact context. It accepts
no opId, target, actor, card, filesystem path or grant. Apply the same candidate
validator and occupancy checks to one coherent read snapshot. Return exact context,
current/candidate definitions, proposed next policyVersion, wouldChange boolean,
structured lane-label/order/add/retire and edge/right differences, current-card
counts per affected lane including archived counts, retained approval count and
the explicit approval-preserved rule. Identify newly effective/suppressed actor
ceilings without exposing credentials or other principals' secret data.
The success shape is exactly
{apiVersion:1,boardId,incarnation,revision,policyVersion,currentWorkflow?:Workflow,
 candidate:Workflow,wouldChange:boolean,nextPolicyVersion:N,
 changes:{renamed:[{laneId,before,after}],orderChanged:boolean,
          added:[WorkflowLane],retired:[WorkflowLane],transitionsChanged:boolean,
          rights:{human:{added:[Right],removed:[Right]},
                  automation:{added:[Right],removed:[Right]}}},
 occupancy:[{laneId,currentCards:N,archivedCards:N}],
 approval:{preservedCurrentCount:N,rule:"retained-issuing-policy"},
 capacity:{policyRecords:N,policyRecordsAfter:N,canonicalPolicyBytesAfter:N,
           maxPolicyRecords:128,maxCanonicalPolicyBytes:8388608}}.
Here N denotes an appropriate nonnegative safe integer except positive policy
versions. nextPolicyVersion and policyRecordsAfter equal current values for
NO_CHANGE. currentWorkflow is omitted only for implicit default1. Added/retired
lanes retain their definition order. Renamed/occupancy entries sort by exact
lane ID and right arrays retain lexical order. occupancy covers every current
lane, currentCards includes archivedCards, and the candidate is returned exactly
as validated. Complete current/candidate edge arrays carry the transition diff
when transitionsChanged is true. No private path or credential is returned.
Invalid/stale previews return the normal bounded refusal taxonomy. Preview has
no durable operation row, policy, activity, card change or allocation of identity.
It does not reserve revisions or authorize save. Save submits the exact candidate
the user previewed with the same expected revisions. The writer validates again.

The existing single writer serializes policy edits and ALL card/grant mutations.
Check authenticated actor/current authority before revealing policy/occupancy,
then exact board/incarnation and expected board revision, then expected policy,
complete candidate and invariant/occupancy checks. Retain existing AUTH/FORBIDDEN,
identity and board-conflict precedence. Stale policy uses POLICY_CONFLICT.
Well-shaped identity/role mutation, occupied retirement, retired-ID reuse or
invalid candidate uses INVALID_INPUT. Do not persist a partial candidate.
The first-default-edit compatibility failure uses WORKFLOW_INCOMPATIBLE as above.
Counters at their safe-integer limit use COUNTER_OVERFLOW with no increment.

On success append one policy record, advance current policy once and board revision
once, record one activity and the original terminal outcome in the SAME transaction.
Change no card/peer revision or approval. On failure write only the permitted
immutable NOT_COMMITTED outcome. No domain/policy/card/activity partial effects.
Preserve pre-freeze authentication/shape/transport refusal handling when there is
no admissible canonical operation identity. Do not invent an outcome for it.
Candidate equality ignores only its required proposed revision. All semantic
fields, exact label bytes, lane order, edge/actor/right arrays and identity match.
After current-authority/CAS/validity admission, equal candidates return durable
NOT_COMMITTED/NO_CHANGE with no counters, version, history or activity advance.
For implicit default1, an otherwise exact original-default candidate is NO_CHANGE
and its draft workflowId is never installed. Never return a made-up committed ID.

Concurrent card move/retirement, edit/approve, edit/grant-revoke and two policy
edits have one writer order. A winner advances board revision. The other's old
envelope refuses as stale before applying. Retried operation identity retains
the first immutable outcome under existing same-op/fingerprint rules. Preserve
the writer's ordering of exact retained-original resolution before fresh command
admission, under current read-own-outcome authority. Do not turn a known original
into a new stale-command refusal. Same opId with changed bytes is the existing
OPERATION_MISMATCH. A prepared
old card request is not rewritten, re-signed, replayed or retroactively evaluated
as its new counterpart. Its exact first-dispatch admission uses current policy
and existing CAS. Completed/failed originals remain exact through later edits.
UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN after preview/current-policy/catalog/search reads.
Only a deliberate authorized lookup of the exact original may reconcile it.
Unavailable/pruned lookup is not permission to replay or invent a terminal result.

6. Ordinary browser and actual CLI
Expose Edit workflow from the selected board for a current authorized editor.
Show semantic roles beside current labels, fixed IDs in details, and protected
review/Completed behavior. Reuse the M06 bounded editor. Add/remove only waiting
lanes, rename/reorder, edit ordinary edges/actors and right ceilings, then explicit
Preview and Save. Show affected occupancy, permission changes and preserved
approvals before Save. Preview is required in the browser flow. Server authority
never relies on the browser having shown it. Invalid save/preview preserves draft.

Actual CLI adds workflow show, workflow preview --input FILE, and
workflow edit --input FILE through the existing board-scoped HTTP transport.
show returns coherent current/history policy context. Preview FILE is the exact
preview body. Edit FILE is the normal full mutation envelope above. JSON/readable
outputs and existing exit taxonomy expose conflicts/NO_CHANGE/UNKNOWN honestly.
CLI performs no implicit read-for-revision, retry, forced save, actor override or
label resolution. Existing card move/start/handoff/approve remain usable with
the profile-aware rules. Demonstrate using the real parser and executable.

Before dispatch, use the existing private prepare/readback/dispatch protocol for
workflow.edit too. Durable private data includes exact bytes/fingerprint/opId,
board/incarnation, expected policy/board versions, candidate and actor binding.
Failure to persist/read back cannot dispatch. UNKNOWN retains that exact original
and draft. Reload, reopen, a second window, changing selection, new previews or
reading current policy never causes auto lookup/replay or clears uncertainty.
Known refusal permits deliberate refreshed editing and a fresh original after
the user sees new context. Never silently transplant edits to a newer policy.

Keep per-window/per-board draft ownership and dirty-discard confirmation. Cancel
preserves until deliberate discard. Escape does not save. Composition Enter never
submits. Multiline controls retain Enter. Explicit keyboard activation of Preview
and Save each dispatches once. Maintain tab order, visible focus, label/role names,
validation focus, and return focus to the exact live opener or documented selected-
board fallback. Delay reads/previews/saves/focus callbacks across board, incarnation,
workspace, project, window and generation changes. Obsolete callbacks must not
paint, overwrite input, move focus or act on a different current selection.
One window's successful edit makes another's original stale, preserving its input
and exact original result rather than doing background rebasing.

7. Existing catalog and Attention compatibility
Keep the M06 catalog/Attention response shapes, authority/freshness distinctions,
committed unarchived-only counting, selected-board identity and known-zero versus
absent/stale/unavailable behavior. Existing Ready for Review count resolves the
current semantic review role ID from the same coherent board snapshot, including
a renamed/reordered default or configured workflow. A literal SQL comparison with
the display label or default string is insufficient on configured boards.
Policy-only edits cannot add/remove cards from this count because the review ID
is fixed. Card and policy concurrent reads still return a coherent revision or
stale/unavailable evidence. The existing UI label may remain Ready for Review.
It names this existing review measure, not a new configurable category.
Preserve lastCardActivity and the catalog ordering derived from it. That existing
measure includes committed card activity only. A workflow.edit has no committed
card revision, so its policy activity does not advance lastCardActivity or reorder
the catalog by that measure. Its board revision and ordinary activity history
still advance as specified.
No new category IDs, assignment, cardinality, counts, configuration, projection
API or category-aware Attention semantics are selected here. M08 owns that
extension and its joined qualification. M07 guides/kit must state this boundary.

8. Physical, generated, API, archive and upgrade compatibility
Select physical user_version9. Keep immutable board.schema_version5, API1,
archive format/schema1, fingerprint1, workspace registry/preferences1 and other
independent versions. PolicyVersion is the monotonically increasing workflow
policy version defined above, no longer fixed1. Generated Go/TypeScript/schema
descriptors include the new command, profile-aware IDs, pins/history/result/refs
and preview shapes. Generation/staging remain authoritative, never hand-edited.
Use distinct policy/workflow revision types and validators. Do not globally widen
the shared Version type and thereby allow other protocol, schema or fingerprint
fields to drift from their independently fixed values.

Freeze physical8's exact DDL/validator independently before current helpers change.
Only add explicit offline8->9, retaining supported5->6->7->8 separate steps.
Current startup requires9 and refuses old/unknown/corrupt stores with explicit
upgrade guidance. Old8 binaries refuse9. Old strict API/archive consumers may
refuse edited objects and must never silently discard policy history/profile.
Untouched M06 default/configured reads and exports retain omitted new fields.

Physical9 retains complete versioned definitions/current pointer and state pins.
An implementation may add versioned policy tables alongside old immutable rows,
but must provide one authoritative validated current policy, no two independently
writable copies. The exact physical8 workflow_definition row, existing rows and
all original values remain conserved evidence. Upgrade seeds policy1 from exact
M06 definition or implicit default and treats existing state pins as implicit1.
No UUID regeneration, JSON snapshot rewrite, lane conversion, grant rewrite,
approval update, label backfill or original re-encoding is allowed on upgrade.
The old default historical/opaque storage validation branch and exact opaque
bytes remain valid through upgrade, later policy editing and reopen. Retain the
API/archive representability boundary stated above. The first-real-edit current-
state compatibility gate is not an upgrade/open gate.
Validate contiguous policy history, fixed IDs/roles, lifetime retired IDs, current
membership and historical membership under the appropriate pins. Refuse gaps,
conflicting current records, unresolvable pins, illegal historical actor rights or
approval provenance. Legitimate historical grants above current ceiling remain
valid stored evidence and confer only current effective rights.

Use M04/M05 offline ownership/private-path/ACL/exclusion/inventory gates, expected
board/incarnation, closed host/lease and BEGIN IMMEDIATE revalidation. Full schema,
FK/integrity and complete-record conservation validation precedes COMMIT. Repeat
upgrade is NO_CHANGE. Precommit refusal leaves valid exact8. Ambiguous durability
is UNKNOWN, never alleged rollback. Successful earlier adjacent steps remain if
a later step refuses. No startup migration or repair, no undocumented direct
5/6/7->9 shortcut and no live SQL tooling as an ordinary editing path.

Archives include complete policy history/current pointer, profile, retired IDs,
state pins and all existing records. Old M06 archives import as policy1 without
fabricated public fields. Validate the whole candidate before target publication.
Missing/foreign policy/pin, wrong profile, role mutation, internally inconsistent
retirement/retired-ID reuse, malformed rights/edge and broken approval refuse safely.
Do not test historical cards or snapshots only against the latest active lanes.
Retained attempted inputs may target an unknown/retired lane and legitimately
record NOT_COMMITTED. They remain valid immutable evidence independent of current
membership. Never reconstruct their canonical bytes from current definitions.

Restore retains policy/workflow/lane IDs and versions, all history/approval facts,
full/compact originals/results/fingerprints, tombstones, blobs and counters.
Only existing explicitly documented board/incarnation/reference rebasing and fresh
local-channel credential/bootstrap transforms apply. Preserve attribution actors,
old origin fields and archived grant/history evidence without turning it into
current authorization. Restored channels get ordinary rights bounded by the
restored CURRENT ceiling. Old hidden grant rights do not become fresh credentials.
Prove full-record comparison at first restore and after new policy/card activity
followed by a second restore, including retired lanes and original UNKNOWN.
Restore success is separate from physical-upgrade proof.

9. Acceptance, ownership and remaining decisions
#0058 owns authoritative storage/domain/API/real-CLI/generated/read/archive/upgrade
implementation and focused admission/conservation tests. #0034 owns ordinary
browser edit/preview/save and pending-original/context/input behavior. #0098 owns
complete native/browser inventories, joined normal journey and two clean builds.
#0099 owns runnable extracted kit, normal demonstration, guide/schema closure and
durable handoff. Shared localpost diagnostics are mandatory for browser work,
using its existing canonical service, private per-run registration/inbox, bounded
schema-safe metadata, direct completed-file inspection and owned cleanup. Do not
replace shared service or weaken product CSP/authentication. Diagnostics failure
never changes product outcomes, clears input or triggers another mutation.

Acceptance must cover each supported edit/refusal on both existing profiles,
historical labels and retired membership, retained approval/prerequisite validity,
ceiling intersection/re-expansion, all-or-nothing racing writers, stale/NO_CHANGE,
old/new canonical originals and UNKNOWN, ordinary browser/actual CLI, current
Attention compatibility, reopen, separate8->9 and complete two-restore comparison.
This design deliberately refuses identity/role replacement and occupied retirement.
Its use of ordinary edit for policy admission and default-profile extension are
explicit owner-review choices. No extra owner-only input is needed to finish the
proposal. Terry's acceptance is the implementation gate. This document grants no
M08 category implementation, real-board migration or wider administration scope.
